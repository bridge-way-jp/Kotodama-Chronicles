# Optional extra graphics (nothing here is needed for the game to run)

Always attach as style reference: `hm_style.webp`, `hm_items_tokyo.webp`, `hm_train_local.webp` (from art/sheets/).
Common start: "16-bit pixel art in exactly the same warm, clean style as the attached images: crisp dark warm-brown outlines, light from the top-left, no blur, no anti-aliasing. Plain pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, no text. Landscape 1536 x 1024."

## 1) Crane-game machine for the word crane game  ->  hm_crane_machine.webp
"A Japanese arcade crane-game machine seen from the front: pink and blue cabinet, glass box, a metal claw hanging in the middle, a pile of round colourful capsules inside, a joystick and two big buttons, a prize chute at the bottom. Draw it once, large (about 700 px tall), centred. Also, to the right, draw the claw alone in three poses: open, closing, closed (each about 120 px tall)."

## 2) Word capsules  ->  hm_capsules.webp
"A sheet of 12 capsule balls (the gashapon kind, two halves, glossy) in 6 colours (pink, blue, green, yellow, orange, purple), two designs per colour: closed, and half open. Each about 96 x 96 px, arranged in a 6 x 2 grid with clear magenta gaps. No text."

## 3) Season and weather particles  ->  hm_particles.webp
"A sheet of small particle sprites, each about 24 x 24 px, in rows: (1) five cherry-blossom petals in different rotations, (2) five autumn leaves (red, orange, yellow), (3) five snowflakes of different sizes, (4) five raindrops (long streaks, slightly blue), (5) five fireflies (soft yellow glow dots with a faint halo). Clear magenta gaps, no text."

## 4) Departure-board skin (optional, only if you want a sprite version of the board)  ->  hm_board.webp
"A Japanese electronic station departure board seen from the front: black panel with a grey frame, amber LED pixel text lines (no readable words, just rows of glowing amber dashes and blocks), a small green and a small blue train-type label on the left of each row, a clock area at the top right. 1400 x 360 px, centred."
