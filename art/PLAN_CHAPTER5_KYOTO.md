# Chapter 5 plan: Kyoto (京都)  — draft for discussion

**Why Kyoto:** the first big city and the first place where tradition and mass tourism collide. After four small places
the player meets crowds, trains with transfers, and indirect, very polite communication.
It is also the first chapter in a modern, realistic city, so it is the template for Osaka and Tokyo.

## Story (short)
Maria follows K's next letter ("in a city where the old and the new walk side by side"). Kyoto's Kotodama are not
forgotten but **drowned out**: signs in five languages, announcements, social-media posts, and so many visitors that
the old shopkeepers stop talking about what they really mean. The region master (grammar) is an old machiya owner and
tea-house keeper who speaks only in hints; the player has to learn to *read between the lines*
(indirect expressions, polite refusals, 本音と建前).

## Society themes (each unlocks Japan notes; keep balanced, show several viewpoints)
- Overtourism: crowded buses, photo etiquette in Gion, locals who feel crowded out vs. shops that live from visitors
- Tradition vs. selling it: kimono rental, machiya turned into guesthouses, craftspeople without successors
- Indirect communication: ぶぶ漬け-style hints (a stereotype; the game shows it as a *story people tell*, not a rule),
  polite refusal, reading the air (空気を読む)
- Newcomers and old residents; everyday modern Japan: transfers, IC card gates, station crowds, queueing

## People
- **Master (grammar):** an old tea-house owner who speaks in hints — region master
- **Machiya host:** a middle-aged woman running a guesthouse in her family house (home base, bed, job)
- **Kimono-rental student:** a part-time worker from abroad or another prefecture (lets the player see a newcomer's view)
- **Bus driver / station staff:** short, funny language lessons about crowds and announcements
- **K:** appears at the end of the chapter, again only partly

## Learning
- Grammar focus: indirect/soft expressions and polite refusal patterns suited to the chapter (N2 level), plus keigo recap
- Vocabulary: about 24 story words (観光公害, 町家, 空気を読む, 遠慮, 本音, 行列 …), extra reading/listening texts
- Jobs: tea house (listening), station information desk (reading signs/announcements)
