## Gemeinsamer Teil (steht schon in jeder Nachricht unten)

```
Create a walking sprite sheet for a top-down 16-bit RPG (like Pokémon Emerald / Stardew Valley), in the same pixel-art style as the attached character sheet. The faces, hair and clothes must match the attached portraits exactly.

RULES (very important):
- Every character has the same body size: chibi proportions, head about 1/3 of the body, all characters exactly the same height (children a bit smaller), all feet on the same baseline.
- Each character is one BLOCK of 4 columns × 4 rows:
  Row 1: facing DOWN (towards the viewer)
  Row 2: facing LEFT (true side profile, only one eye visible)
  Row 3: facing RIGHT (true side profile, only one eye visible; draw it, do not just mirror, bags and items stay on the same body side as in the other views)
  Row 4: facing UP (back view: back of the head and the back of the same clothes, no face)
- The 4 frames in each row are a calm walk cycle: standing, left foot forward, standing, right foot forward. Only legs and arms move; head, hair and clothes stay identical between frames.
- The same outfit, colours and accessories in every direction. Nothing appears or disappears between views.
- Clean dark warm-brown outline, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no ground shadow, no text, no labels, no large preview figure.
- Leave a clear gap between all frames and a bigger gap between characters. 3 characters side by side, 2 rows of characters.
- Background: plain pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere. Landscape 1536 × 1024.
```
