# Zug-Grafik für die Zugfahrt-Szene

Zu jedem Prompt anhängen (Ordner Referenz/):
- aktuelle_bus_szene_fertig.png: so sieht die fertige Szene mit dem Bus aus (Stil, Größe, Horizont)
- aktuelle_zug_szene_platzhalter.png: so ist der Zug jetzt (nur Platzhalter aus Code), er soll an dieselbe Stelle
- hm_bus_Stil_und_Massstab.webp: Pixel-Art-Stil und Detailgrad des Busses
- hw_road.webp und hw_mid.webp: Hintergrund, vor dem der Zug fährt

Gemeinsamer Anfang (in jede Nachricht kopieren):
"16-bit pixel art in exactly the same warm, clean style as the attached bus sheet: crisp dark warm-brown outlines, light from the top-left, no blur, no anti-aliasing. Side view of a Japanese train, facing RIGHT. Plain pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, no ground, no tracks, no shadows, no text, no people, no passengers. Landscape 1536 x 1024."

Draw THREE separate pieces on the sheet, stacked vertically with clear gaps: (1) the HEAD CAR facing right with the long nose at the right end, about 640 px wide, (2) a MIDDLE CAR with flat ends, about 640 px wide, (3) the TAIL CAR (the nose faces left, mirrored, so it can end the train) about 640 px wide. All three exactly the same height and baseline, windows aligned so the cars connect seamlessly, the bogies visible below."

Draw TWO separate pieces on the sheet, stacked with a clear gap: (1) one single railcar facing right, about 700 px wide, (2) the same railcar seen from the same side but with doors open (to show a boarding scene), same size and baseline."
