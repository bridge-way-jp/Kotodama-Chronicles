# New valley ground map (easier to walk)

**Attach:** hm_style.webp, ground_valley_draft.webp

```
16-bit top-down RPG pixel art in the same warm style and 3/4 top-down view as the attached images. The first attached image is the style sheet. The second attached image is the CURRENT map of the Hinomori valley (peach orchard valley with a hot-spring inn): keep its mood, colours (autumn, peach blossom, warm light) and scenery, but REDRAW it so that it is much easier to WALK through in a game with a 32-px tile grid. Paint ONLY THE GROUND, no buildings, no people, no text, filling the whole picture edge to edge as one map. Landscape 1536 × 1024 (42 × 28 tiles, so 1 tile = about 36.6 px).

WALKING RULES (very important):
- Every path is WIDE: at least 3 tiles (about 110 px) wide, drawn as clearly visible sandy dirt or stone paving. No one-tile footpaths, no narrow ledges.
- Paths run STRAIGHT and in right angles (horizontal or vertical, rounded corners are fine). NO diagonal paths and NO diagonal bridges.
- The bridge over the river is straight, horizontal or vertical, and 3 tiles wide.
- Open areas the player can walk on are plain, flat and free of obstacles: a big OPEN VILLAGE SQUARE in the middle (about 8 × 6 tiles) paved with stone, with only a small stone well or a lantern at its very edge, NO tree or flowerbed in the middle of it.
- Steps (stairs) between levels are 3 tiles wide.
- Everything that should block (river, cliffs, dense forest, vineyard and rice terraces, rocks) is clearly different from the paths: darker, with a visible edge.

LAYOUT (percent of picture width × height, left/top = 0):
- Bottom middle (x 46–54 %): the entrance road from the south edge up to the square (a short asphalt road at the very bottom edge, then paving). A small bus stop plot beside the road at about x 52–62 %, y 60–66 %.
- Centre (x 36–64 %, y 46–70 %): the open village square.
- Upper left (x 8–22 %, y 12–26 %): flat plot with a small yard for a thatched farmhouse, with a wide path leading to the square and a small peach grove next to it. A small word-garden plot (6 × 4 tiles of soil beds, walkable around) beside it.
- Upper middle (x 32–60 %, y 4–26 %): a vineyard on terraces (blocked, no paths inside), with a wide path along its bottom edge.
- Upper right (x 58–80 %, y 3–16 %): a peach grove (walkable clearing in the middle, tall grass patch where wild creatures live).
- Right (x 66–88 %, y 40–58 %): a large flat plot for a hot-spring inn (about 7 × 3 tiles) with an open stone-paved onsen square (about 7 × 5 tiles) in front of it to the south, and a small hot-spring pool with steam at the far right edge (blocked).
- Left of the square (x 36–46 %, y 46–54 %): a small flat plot (3 × 2 tiles) for a calligraphy workshop, right next to the path.
- Below the square (x 34–44 %, y 66–72 %): a small plot (3 × 2 tiles) for a fruit stand; the winery plot (4 × 2 tiles) at x 50–62 %, y 44–52 %.
- A river flows from the top right, runs along the left of the picture and ends in a small lake at the lower left (blocked), with ONE straight 3-tile wide wooden bridge crossing it in the upper left-middle and a small fishing spot (tall grass, walkable) on the lower-left bank.
- Rice terraces in the lower right (x 68–96 %, y 62–95 %), blocked, with a wide path along their top edge.
- All edges of the map: dense forest and cliffs (blocked), except the south road.
Paint NO buildings, NO people, NO text.
```
